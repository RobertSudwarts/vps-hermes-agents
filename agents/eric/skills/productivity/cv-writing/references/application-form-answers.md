# Application-form answers

Keep the CV unchanged unless Rob separately requests an edit. Use the exact question, all its subparts, and the stated word/character/sentence limit. Count against the specified unit; obey one- or two-sentence requests literally. If a missing question or limit prevents compliance, recover it or ask narrowly.

Select complementary supported examples across questions, not the same whole-CV summary for every field. Reuse canonical evidence and confirmations already available; retrieve only missing relevant facts. Draft concise first-person text for Rob to review and adopt.

## Match evidence to the question

- **Build/challenge:** identify the complete product, purpose and a meaningful engineering challenge. Preserve confirmed asynchronous processing, multi-user scope and analytical work rather than reducing a system to summarisation. Architecture alone does not establish what Rob personally found hardest; obtain that fact or flag proposed framing separately.
- **Initiative/result:** state what Rob started and its evidenced result or current operating state. Do not invent adoption, paying users, revenue, measured gains or specific concurrency incidents when only implementation is known.
- **AI workflow:** distinguish AI-assisted development from AI embedded in the product. Describe supported requirements, completion criteria and review practice. Do not infer a named assistant, repository workflow, CI gate, daily cadence, recurring AI failure or productivity metric from general AI use. Software tests/human validation do not establish systematic model or agent evaluation.
- **Motivation/values:** connect the work to a supported contribution and show each requested value through actual conduct. Applying does not establish personal admiration, ideological commitment or a reason for leaving. Keep proposed motivation clearly marked outside the answer for Rob's review; “briefly” usually calls for a compact paragraph, not a cover letter.
- **Digital assets/trading:** distinguish software supporting an algorithm, oversight of systematic strategies and personally researched prediction-market applications. Preserve prototype purpose and operating state without implying live execution, returns, market-making, on-chain implementation or unconfirmed mechanisms.
- **Qualification/proficiency screening:** answer the exact factual question directly. Do not substitute adjacent experience for the requested credential, years, ownership or proficiency; relevance does not turn a gap into a yes.

Ask one narrow question when an essential personal detail is unknown; otherwise draft only the supported portion and explain the unresolved part outside employer-facing prose. Never fill a narrative gap with invented difficulty, motivation or outcome.

## Save, check and retry

Use one existing working answers file with exact question/answer pairs and short separate provenance/framing notes. Verify every requested subpart and length, not just the presence of technologies. No per-question dossiers or archive layers.

A retry updates and returns only the requested answer at the requested length. Preserve unrelated answers and the CV. Record new confirmations with provenance. Drafted or delivered answers are not evidence of final pasted answers; use [application records](application-records.md) only when a submission is reported.
