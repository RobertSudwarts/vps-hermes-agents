# Substantive CV drafting

## Establish the target and evidence

1. Start with Rob's selected vacancy/specification and existing employer research, fit assessment and open questions. Do not broaden tailoring into job discovery. Recover missing specification details before relying on them; research only claims needing verification.
2. Identify the actual work, essential requirements and optional examples. A mandatory form question is a screening uncertainty, not automatically a job requirement. Exclude roles explicitly requiring professional German; flag preferred or unspecified German as uncertain. Rob chooses opportunities; do not quietly substitute a different role.
3. Read relevant canonical records and existing confirmed supplements before asking Rob to repeat history. Map priority needs privately to **direct evidence**, **transferable evidence**, and **gaps/unknowns**. Missing wording is not proof of missing ability. Ask only unresolved questions that change a claim or the hiring case; batch them and stop once the substance is established.
4. Keep short claim-to-source pointers and dated/contextual confirmations in existing application notes. Separate employer facts, career facts and editorial interpretation. Generated CVs may guide presentation or accepted wording, but cannot independently establish career facts. If sources conflict, resolve the particular claim or omit it pending clarification.

## Calibrate claims

- Tool confirmation permits listing the tool, not inferring years, proficiency, production scale, implementation depth or employer/product attribution. Reassess the remaining gap. A technology check covers tools in this specification, distinguishing required, optional, confirmed and unknown items.
- Preserve formal titles, dates, qualifications and historical approval scope. Partial study or experience is not an equivalent degree; delivery practice is not certification; historical regulatory approval is not an examination or current authorisation.
- Preserve personal delivery versus accountable team ownership. Describe leadership by workstream, location and period; distinguish developers from delivery managers. Do not sum teams into concurrent/direct-report totals, or invent sequential timing to avoid that sum. In BofA narrative prose use Rob's accepted **a lead developer**, retaining the formal title.
- Keep cross-career totals, counterparties and responsibilities at aggregate scope unless employer attribution is confirmed. Fund-risk accountability, software-supported portfolios and assets under management measure different things; none automatically proves engineering impact.
- Adjacent experience does not establish specialist ownership: application LLM integration is not GPU/model infrastructure; software tests or human validation are not systematic LLM evaluation; general cloud use is not named-service/platform expertise. Likewise, monitoring use does not prove alert configuration. Check the mechanism actually evidenced.
- Do not turn limited exposure into either expertise or zero experience. Both positive and negative capability claims need support. Hold pending confirmations when Rob requests a later consolidated revision; canonical-source maintenance is a separate task.

## Choose the hiring argument

State what Rob could take responsibility for and which evidence earns it. The objective is “Yes, we need him”, not a promise of employer interest or a matched-keyword inventory. Reconsider omitted roles when they add distinct evidence; do not inherit another application's emphasis.

Choose positioning for the work:

- **Engineering leadership:** lead with current personal implementation, delivery, production responsibility and supported leadership scope. Keep seniority visible without making a hands-on candidate sound detached from engineering.
- **Financial/product ownership:** where evidenced, connect accountable business decisions with engineering: assets, investment strategies, trading/execution, financing, risk and operational controls. Read actual mandates and approvals; do not reduce investment expertise to incidental finance exposure or generic software correctness. For institutional products, map onboarding, counterparties, margin/financing and execution-to-reconciliation to actual ownership. A missing PM title does not erase relevant responsibility; workflow ownership does not invent product strategy or a portfolio-management mandate.
- **Technical business analysis:** lead with translating trading/risk needs into requirements, workflows, design and validation; use implementation as supporting evidence. Financial derivatives experience does not establish physical-commodity logistics or named trading-platform implementation.

These are conditional lenses, not a finance template. Select the strongest role-specific combination and keep unsupported specialist gaps in private assessment.

For a **focused open application**, propose bounded work grounded in verified employer context. Calibrate the same three substantive sections to that contribution. Employer need remains a hypothesis unless verified; invent no vacancy, title, team, reporting line or headcount. Do not borrow requirements from a different specialist advert. Market-maker oversight is not personally building a market maker; fund-risk controls are not a liquidation engine. Pair relevant older financial ownership with recent hands-on work when that supports the proposal.

## Construct the Markdown

Follow [the Markdown output contract](markdown-format.md) before approval so the
saved draft is compatible with the approved builder. If Rob requests a different
structure, resolve that explicitly rather than silently changing it in production.

Use About me, Skills, Experience and Education in that order unless Rob requests otherwise:

- **About me:** a plain factual narrative of contribution, scope and evidence. Avoid synthetic specialist labels and adjective-heavy biography. Financial ownership may lead for investment work; engineering may lead for engineering work. A newly confirmed fact does not automatically belong in the opening.
- **Skills:** select, group and order capabilities for this role, including relevant domain and leadership skills. Omit distracting minor exposure rather than replacing honest limitations with inflated synonyms.
- **Experience:** tailor each selected role to substantiate the opening. Preserve reverse chronology; label the history as selected if omissions could suggest unexplained gaps. Fresh bullets, combinations and paraphrases are allowed when meaning, attribution and scope remain traceable.
- **Education:** accurate, concise qualifications, without invented equivalences or unnecessary old detail.

For builder/founder work, make distinct products and their purposes recognisable before listing technology. Use application-led bullets with implementation attached to the correct product. Preserve evidenced asynchronous/multi-user scope and analytical purpose; do not reduce a complete application to summarisation. Keep research/prototype status accurate: arbitrage analysis does not establish live-money execution, a specific mechanism or realised returns. Do not transfer one product's tools or live-update mechanism to another.

Remove repetition and feature tours; use metrics only when supported and useful. Each section should do a different job: profile proposes, skills expose capabilities, experience proves. Keep factual gaps and provenance outside employer-facing text, while answering explicit screening questions honestly.

Save `working/cv.md`, keep brief evidence/uncertainty notes alongside existing working records, and apply the [review checklist](review-checklist.md). Show the complete Markdown for a new draft or requested rewrite. A page target can guide selection, but actual page fit belongs to later PDF production; do not claim it from Markdown or cut approved text to fit without review.
