# Employer-side editorial review

Apply before presenting a substantive Markdown draft. Review a local edit only at the scope affected.

- **First screen:** using only the opening and prominent evidence, can a reader identify the proposed responsibility, the experience earning it, whether Rob is hands-on/a leader/a specialist, and why that combination helps this employer? Repair missing connections with better evidence and prose, not a longer adjective-heavy summary.
- **Section calibration:** About me states the contribution; Skills selects relevant capabilities and depth; each Experience entry proves a distinct part; Education remains factual. A tailored opening above generic experience is unfinished. Keep the first three complementary rather than repetitive.
- **Claim audit:** trace significant claims to source or confirmation. Check titles, dates, chronology, scope, personal/team attribution, qualifications and mechanisms. Do not assign current tools to older roles, cross-career totals to one employer, or multiple teams to an unsupported concurrent headcount. Check meaning, not verbatim identity with source sentences.
- **Selection:** retain strong recent implementation and enough older domain ownership to establish the chosen combination. Can the reader name the products, their purpose and supported implementation? Remove redundant bullets, irrelevant education and keywords with no distinct evidential value.
- **Fit and voice:** distinguish direct and transferable evidence from gaps. Keep internal caveats outside the CV without asserting missing qualifications. Replace superlatives, defensive disclaimers and bureaucratic inventories with concrete responsibility. For open applications, make the bounded contribution clear without implying a vacancy or specialist remit.

If the draft only rearranges old text without improving the hiring argument, revise the prose before presenting a new final draft. If Rob rejects the positioning, compare an accepted passage and revise the affected section in plain text before another full-document iteration. Do not modify skills automatically in response to feedback.

An internal editorial assessment is not recruiter feedback, employer interest, Rob's text approval or PDF approval. Report genuine unresolved factual constraints with the draft; do not claim hiring outcomes.
