# Employer-side CV review

Before presenting a substantive Markdown draft, check:

- **Hiring argument:** Can a reader identify the specific contribution Rob could make and why his evidence supports it?
- **Evidence:** Is every significant claim traceable to a career source or confirmation? Are responsibility, scope and uncertainty represented honestly?
- **Selection:** Does each section add distinct evidence, with recent implementation and relevant domain experience balanced for the role?
- **Fit:** Are genuine gaps retained in private assessment rather than hidden by inflated wording?
- **Voice:** Is the prose concrete, professional and concise without generic superlatives or defensive disclaimers?

An internal review is not recruiter feedback and is not evidence of employer interest.
