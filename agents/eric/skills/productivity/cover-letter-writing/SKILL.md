# Cover-letter writing

Use for a cover letter, motivation letter or application statement for a named role or a focused open application. It explains mutual fit; it is not a CV rewritten as paragraphs.

Use the role specification, the relevant career evidence and, where necessary, verified employer context. State the useful contribution Rob could make, why the work matters to him, and evidence that supports the case. Do not invent employer admiration, a vacancy, a team, a title, personal motivation or unverified qualifications.

Keep the letter separate from CV drafting. A letter request does not authorise a CV rewrite, PDF build, application, outreach or external upload. When both CV and letter are requested, reuse the same evidence and avoid duplicating the full career narrative.

Deliver a Markdown draft for review unless Rob explicitly asks for a separate approved-document production step.
