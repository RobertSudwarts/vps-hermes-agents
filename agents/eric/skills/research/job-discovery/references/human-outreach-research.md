# Requested human-route research and drafts

Use only for requested recruiter/hiring-manager research, relationship or
screenshot interpretation, or an outreach draft. It does not authorise sending,
sign-in or renewed access to paused accounts.

1. Check existing employer research, applications and confirmed correspondence
   for prior contact names, messages and replies. New requisitions/people do not
   reset relationship history. Ask narrowly if missing history would change the
   message; a pending connection request does not prove whether a message was sent.
2. Find people closest to the actual work. Rank a small set by evidenced remit,
   separating plausible contacts from the confirmed recruiter/hiring manager for
   the exact requisition. Verify current remit and departures using recent dated
   professional evidence; a senior HR title or old appointment is insufficient.
3. Prefer employer material, self-published professional routes or public recruiting
   announcements. Indexed profiles/mirrors are attributed leads, not employer
   confirmation. Never guess corporate addresses or substitute contact-broker
   data. A self-published personal-provider address inviting relevant contact can
   be valid; retain purpose/date of inboxes, and do not repurpose fraud-reporting
   channels as recruitment enquiries or assume published mailboxes are monitored.
4. Inspect supplied screenshots before proposing next steps. Separate visible
   wording/status/date from Rob's report; leave cropped text unknown. Silence does
   not prove a read, rejection motive or delivery. Paid messaging does not assure
   attention. Sent wording is correspondence evidence, not automatically career fact.
5. Draft for the actual relationship stage. A first introduction connects relevant
   work and location with one modest request. After an unanswered introduction,
   prefer a short routing follow-up or genuinely different contact rather than
   repeating the full pitch. A mutual badge is not a warm relationship. Recommend
   one channel first, not simultaneous duplicate messages.
6. Keep framing constructive. Do not invent ATS/AI/discrimination explanations,
   promise an interview or shelve an employer Rob wants to pursue. A broader HR
   request calls for a different evidenced route. Save confirmed relationship
   history with employer research and show the draft for Rob to send.

Reverify people, vacancies and relevant claims before use. A request to inspect a
public vacancy or Rob's own profile does not permit contacts/messages/profile
sweeps. Avoid substantial unpaid work before an agreed need and scope exist.
