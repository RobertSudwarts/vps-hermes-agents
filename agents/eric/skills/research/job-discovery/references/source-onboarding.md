# Source onboarding

Use only when adding or changing a job source, adapter or parser.

Confirm the source is public and relevant to the active brief. Define its URL, update cadence, data fields, pagination/completeness limits and failure semantics before relying on it. Preserve source IDs and raw observations; a partial or failed response is unknown, not evidence that a role closed.

Test collection against a small known sample before adding it to routine scans. Do not install software, store credentials, change schedules or scrape authenticated/paused accounts without explicit permission.
