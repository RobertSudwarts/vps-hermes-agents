# Bounded discovery and role assessment

## Set the pass

Read the active brief, relevant register/application state and exact prior ID/URL
exclusions. Declare source/employer scope, date and a maximum substantive-review
count. Separate cheap screening from full assessment and keep one deduplicated
set of exact postings. If parallel work is explicitly authorised, divide this
same cap and disjoint scope across workers; give compact evidence packets and
exclusions. Reassign only unfinished allocations after failure, preserving
verified work. No automatic delegation or multiplied review caps.

Refresh known sources via [incremental search](incremental-search.md) when
needed. Supplement them with bounded searches for unfamiliar employers, unusual
titles and Austria-compatible remote work. Source bodies stay on disk. Do not
pad to the cap, count inaccessible adverts as completed assessments, or call an
unseen backlog newly published. When using old backlog because new/changed roles
are unsuitable, say so and record why the changed records were not selected.

For employer-universe expansion, use sector directories and primary company
sites; regulatory registers can seed candidates. Preserve IDs/categories/local
addresses and deduplicate legal entities transparently. Separate domestic licence,
local establishment/representative office and cross-border passporting: the last
is not local employer substance. Verify business, local functions and careers
route before treating a candidate as an active source. A register/watchlist is
not a census. Record actual recruitment-portal URLs, filters and coverage;
general web queries or individual employer feeds are not platform-wide searches.

## Screen, assess, deepen

- Screen actual status, geography, language, grade and role-defining work before
  deep research. Exclude mandatory professional German; preferred/unspecified
  German remains uncertain, not excluded. Apply language gates to exact roles,
  including distinct consulting and international-product divisions.
- Read the substantive primary specification before a current recommendation;
  recheck it before resurfacing an old recommendation. Use `grounded-citations`
  for vacancy evidence and recovery. Saved-state reporting is not a live check.
- Compare actual career sources and confirmed supplements: direct evidence,
  transferable evidence and real gaps. Missing wording is not missing ability.
  Preserve personal versus team scope, financial ownership versus familiarity,
  instrument knowledge versus specialist implementation, and application LLM
  engineering versus ML/GPU infrastructure. Never invent expertise or tenure.
- Keep unstated compensation, contract, language and seniority unknown. An
  otherwise strong foreign-office/hybrid role is conditional until Austria-based
  engagement is confirmed. Office lists, English adverts and remote flags do not
  resolve eligibility. Read exact mandatory/optional qualifiers.
- Deepen business context and application questions only for promising roles;
  do not require a full dossier or fully exercised application form for every
  weak lead. Distinguish an active listing from a verified application route.

## Compare roles at one employer

Enumerate distinct IDs/titles/locations from the observed official board/feed
before selecting bodies. Reconcile totals/pagination; if incomplete, do not claim
an employer-wide best fit. Report titles screened separately from full specs read.

Compare exact variants: Senior/Staff, product/engineering and location versions
may have different gates. “Docker or Kubernetes” is not Kubernetes alone;
willingness to switch language is not required prior mastery. Preserve title/body
grade conflicts. Rank actual mandate: acquisition/monetisation PM work is not
interchangeable with technical-product delivery; LLM apps do not prove production
agent platforms or autonomous execution.

Assess mutual value: evidenced finance/workflow ownership plus implementation,
and potential authority, colleagues, resources and development. If asked for one
role, lead with one exact title/link, decisive difference from closest alternatives
and central reservation. Best relative target is not full qualification or Rob's
selection. Say whether the earlier recommendation survives; preserve dated reasons.
Respect a different user selection without repeatedly arguing the ranking.

## Named vacancy versus open application

Retrieve both exact official routes and preserve IDs, geography, entity and listed
state. A general form is a speculative introduction, not funded headcount,
a screening bypass or better response odds. Distinguish platform presence from
Austria payroll/contract capability. Keep inconsistent template wording uncertain.

Check specialist requirements without converting trading/risk knowledge into
live market-making, liquidation engines or on-chain implementation. For an open
route propose a bounded contribution grounded in verified business and Rob's
actual responsibilities; do not invent a team, title or internal problem.
After Rob selects, hand off the target/specification or contribution hypothesis,
primary evidence, fit and open questions. Do not write a differently targeted CV
or apply by implication.

## Record and report

Use [register rules](register-rules.md) for every reviewed role, concrete reasons,
primary evidence, exact user choices, pauses and application links. Keep employer
evaluations separate. Anonymous agency cross-postings with unresolved identity
are related leads, not independently new roles.

Save a dated pass report with scope, completeness warnings and programmatically
reconciled screened/assessed/recommended/conditional/excluded/pending counts.
Label revisits and new requisitions at known employers; preserve relationship
history. Return a small useful shortlist with decisive fit, material reservation,
exact primary source and observation date. Do not pad to a requested upper bound.
For platform coverage, distinguish validated active sources from observed
candidates, and vendor brands from adapter variants.
