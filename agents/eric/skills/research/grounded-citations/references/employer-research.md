# Employer research and mutual value

## Match depth to the request

For a landscape, expansion or career question, lead with products/customers,
revenue model, distribution, stated changes and capabilities being built. Locate
leadership, teams, entity and likely work from evidence; keep budgets, ownership
and internal plans unknown where unverified. Do not replace a strategy question
with an unsolicited misconduct investigation. For vacancy sweeps, verify exact
postings first and deepen only the strongest matches.

Keep dated findings, ledger, evidence and short navigation under
`/vault/eric/Research/<topic>/`. Separate published facts, attributed company
positioning, career-fit analysis and proposed contributions. Research is not
representation or authority to modify protected career records. Inspect evidence
before writing even working notes; use [ledger workflow](ledger-workflow.md).

Connect a published change to a plausible business problem, likely owning function,
Rob's confirmed financial/workflow and engineering evidence, and a bounded useful
contribution. Also assess what he could gain: authority, resources, colleagues,
learning and proximity to decisions. Those are hypotheses until established;
ask who controls priorities, compliance vetoes, resources and escalation. Do not
infer dysfunction or promised authority from an executive appointment or expansion.

## Expansion and hiring campaigns

- Reconcile saved distinct posting IDs and normalized titles separately; location
  copies/formatting variants do not prove separate funded seats. Group functions
  into commercial, product/trading, engineering/data/infrastructure and support.
- Compare diagnostic specifications with published strategy/product changes.
  Distinguish planned products from live businesses; a multi-product advert is not
  proof of a new exchange or incremental headcount. Repetition proves neither
  growth intent nor ghost jobs.
- Keep brand, subsidiary and employing entity scope separate. One entity's staff,
  accounts, permissions or execution policy does not describe the entire group.
  Commercial dealer-risk/P&L work differs from independent risk oversight and
  investor portfolio management.
- Lead with operating picture and strongest bounded interpretation, then questions
  about funded mandate, team, decision authority, live/planned platform and employer.
  Retain verified material adverse facts proportionately.

## Legitimacy and employment risk, when relevant

1. Separate existence, diversification strategy, local substance and employment
   conditions. A regulated business may still offer an unsuitable role. Distinguish
   underwriting from distribution/platform activity; verify new products' team,
   funding, permissions, partners and launch state. Cross-selling economics is a
   hypothesis unless supported.
2. Match exact legal entities, jurisdictions and permission categories in official
   directories. Insurance-intermediary approval does not imply payments, investment
   advice or trading permissions. A European subsidiary/passporting right does not
   establish Austrian payroll or an EOR. Compare global distributed hiring with a
   substantive regional business; vacancy remit remains uncertain without evidence.
3. Read original adverse notices/disclosures and the company's response. Preserve
   entity, case, event/resolution dates, findings, terms and follow-up. Deduplicate
   accounts of the same case; distinguish allegations, findings, penalties and
   subsequent approvals. Company remediation claims are not exoneration; later
   unrelated approval does not erase an incident. Do not transfer US findings to
   European subsidiaries or present limited research as a solvency/full-history audit.
4. Inspect role terms: attendance versus remote labels, assessment workload/urgency,
   employment type, time-zone overlap, product maturity and accountability versus
   authority. Clarify these before substantial unpaid work or application effort.
5. Attribute employee reports by date, platform, role and location. Include relevant
   positive accounts and concerns; operational teams are not evidence of the target
   engineering team. Publish ratings/counts only if retrieved; label excerpt-only
   coverage. Treat profitability/runway statements as speaker claims absent examined
   financial evidence.
6. End with bounded judgement, strongest verified concerns, unknowns and useful
   diligence questions. Avoid both unsupported scam labels and reassurance based
   solely on registration. Rejection/silence does not establish ATS bias, hidden
   motives or inability; actual feedback is evidence, speculation is not.

## Preserve and deliver

Audit claim support, evidence paths, literal units and entity scope as well as
citation integrity. Count vacancies from saved deduplicated records. Deliver the
interpretation, material gaps and artifact location, not the entire dossier.

Keep dated dossiers immutable as observations; record later changes explicitly.
Reverify named people, roles and products before outreach. A request to read Rob's
profile is not permission to explore contacts/messages. A generic open form may
use the same Talent Team; do not invent a screening bypass.

Relocation is separately scoped maintenance: inventory relative paths/SHA-256,
refuse occupied destinations, compare after moving, update mutable navigation
and check citations/evidence links. Remove an old directory only when empty and
authorised; do not keep competing authoritative copies or rewrite frozen history.
