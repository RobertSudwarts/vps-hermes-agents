# Exact vacancy verification

Inspect the actual primary posting body and attach facts to its exact URL/ID,
not a similar title, employer-wide description, search snippet or another location
variant. Save evidence and retrieval time; use [the ledger workflow](ledger-workflow.md)
for resulting research claims. Recover a shell with [public ATS recovery](public-ats-recovery.md)
only when needed. A full employer dossier is not a prerequisite for screening.

Check and retain:

- Responsibilities, essential versus optional qualifications, title/body grade,
  exact location, attendance, contract, language and compensation. Read the
  substantive `JobPosting`/ATS body rather than related-role cards or boilerplate.
  Intern/Student wording or mandatory enrolment may contradict an Engineer title;
  distinguish a hard gate from an ambiguous label or equivalent-experience route.
- **German:** exclude explicit mandatory professional German. Retain otherwise
  strong roles with preferred/unspecified German and flag uncertainty briefly;
  do not require proof of English-only work. English advert text does not prove
  workplace language. Apply this to each vacancy, not the whole employer.
- **Geography:** company offices and generic remote labels do not prove Austria
  eligibility. Inspect primary/secondary locations and country/attendance terms.
  A strong foreign-office/hybrid role is conditional until cross-border engagement
  is confirmed. A local advert does not establish the employing entity, payroll,
  EOR, contractor arrangement or authority to serve local customers.
- **Compensation:** preserve literal units, currency and period. Separate collective
  minima from offers, daily rates from salary, cash from equity/tokens. Do not
  annualise using assumed billable days, expand an unspecified dollar currency or
  supply a missing period. Flag conflicting hourly/daily or weekly-hours fields.
- **Evidence boundary:** employer requirements do not establish Rob's skills.
  Fit assessment belongs to `job-discovery`, using career sources/confirmations.
  Financial ownership is not merely familiarity, but neither proves a specialist
  payment/trading engine; a tool listing does not prove production years or depth.

## Availability and access

A substantive current listing is an advertised role, not confirmation of funded
headcount or an unfilled position. Record publication date separately from first
observation/retrieval. Check closure/expiry cues and the normal application route
where available. Inspect redirects and final body: HTTP 200 on a generic board or
error landing page is not a live exact vacancy. If only Apply fails/redirects while
the specification remains, retain an unverified route rather than infer closure.
An exact 410 is explicit removal evidence; a refused API request is not.

Keep active listing, role-linked Apply URL, rendered form and tested interactive
form distinct. Do not claim the last two from a feed/HTTP result. Do not submit,
authenticate or reconfigure an unavailable browser to test a route. A supplied
public vacancy permits scoped public retrieval, not profile visits, credential
probes or resumption of paused account access.

Failed, partial or empty retrieval means unknown; never silently fill gaps from
memory. Preserve contradictory metadata instead of picking the convenient field.
Recheck before a new/current recommendation or resurfacing an old one; saved-state
pipeline reports retain their last-observed dates and need no automatic refresh.
