# Grounded citations

Use this skill when an external claim matters to a job recommendation, employer assessment, CV, cover letter or application answer.

Prefer the primary vacancy, employer site, regulator, company filing or other first-party source. Record the URL, retrieval date, relevant excerpt or field, and what it establishes. Do not present a search snippet, an inaccessible page title or model memory as verification.

Keep source evidence separate from Rob's protected career record. External evidence may support a statement about an employer or role; it never authorises an invented claim about Rob.

Use concise inline links or a compact source list in delivered research. Keep bulk page text and retrieval logs in files, not recurring model context. If the source is blocked, incomplete or contradictory, report the uncertainty rather than filling the gap.
